# Task: generate leading questions

Produce a fresh set of leading questions for Grandpa's home screen — prompts to help him pick his next recording topic.

## Output format

A plain markdown list of 6-10 questions. Output the list only — no preamble, no closing, no "here are the questions" framing, no code fences.

Each item is a single question. No sub-bullets, no explanations.

## Guidance

- Draw from the recording summaries (what he's already covered) and the bio (what periods or themes exist to explore).
- Favor questions that open a story. "What was the house on Elm Street like when you were small?" — not "Did you grow up on Elm Street?"
- Mix categories:
  - **Follow-ups** on recent threads ("You mentioned your uncle Frank last time — what else do you remember about him?")
  - **New territory** he hasn't covered ("You haven't said much about your Navy years — what was basic training like?")
  - **Sensory or specific details** rather than abstract ones ("What did Sunday dinner smell like at your grandmother's?")
- Don't repeat topics already well-covered in the recording summaries.
- Vary the eras across the list — don't cluster all in childhood or all in career.
- Name-drop specific people, places, and events from his bio or summaries where it sharpens the prompt.
